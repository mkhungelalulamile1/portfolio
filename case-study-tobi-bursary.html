CONVERSATIONAL UX · SERVICE DESIGN

A support conversation built to move people forward.

A documented conversational-interface concept for an IT helpdesk. The work explores a guided troubleshooting flow that replaces vague support requests with clear, step-by-step help.

ProjectIT helpdesk assistant
ContributionConversation design · UX / UI design · prototype direction
EvidenceDocumented interface work
Evidence-led case study.The visuals on this page are supplied project artefacts. Commercial outcomes are not presented as facts where they are not publicly verified.
01 · Frame

Start with the decision.

How can a support conversation establish context, offer a useful next action and know when the person needs a different route?

02 · Explore

Make the work visible.

The project evidence is shown as a small, inspectable gallery rather than decorative filler. Open an image to examine the interface direction.

03 · Decide

Turn insight into interface rules.

  • Use one clear question or action at a time so the conversation remains easy to follow on a phone.
  • Write guidance in plain language and keep the recovery path visible when a suggested fix does not work.
  • Treat escalation as part of the designed experience rather than a dead end.
04 · Deliver

Design for the build.

A documented mobile conversation direction with a supporting process flow and application-screen reference.
