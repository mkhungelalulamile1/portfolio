Independent case study · B2B fintech concept

Make accounts receivable visible before it becomes urgent.

A B2B cash-collection workspace that gives finance teams one shared view of invoices, commitments and follow-up.

Product strategy · workflow design · dashboard UI · component specificationConcept work · not a client claim

Original interface concept created for this portfolio case study — visual product artefacts, not AI imagery.

01 · Opportunity

A specific problem, not a feature list.

A growing business can know its revenue and still be surprised by cash flow. LedgerLane makes the next collection action obvious while keeping the customer history close to the decision.

Prototype targeta finance lead can identify the highest-risk follow-ups at a glance.
Prototype targetan account manager can record an agreed next step in a single flow.
02 · Process

From question to testable direction.

The case study follows an end-to-end product process designed to make assumptions visible before a build begins.

01Workflow mapping
02Information architecture

03 · Decisions

Design principles that shape the flow.

  • Turn a long receivables list into a prioritised action queue.
  • Keep invoice status, relationship context and next action together in one operational view.
  • Design for finance leads and account managers without making either role work in a foreign system.
04 · Build

Built for a real frontend team.

The interface direction defines responsive hierarchy, reusable patterns and explicit interaction states. A production handoff would add component specifications, content rules, accessibility requirements and analytics events before development starts.

UIVisual hierarchy and responsive layouts
UXRoles, decisions and state transitions
FEComponents, states and interaction contract
05 · Validate

What I would test next.

Recruit people who match the intended role, test the central decision in a clickable prototype, then use those observations to revise the flow before implementation. Outcomes here are deliberately presented as prototype targets — not results.
